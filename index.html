0 · Abstractproblem · approach · contribution
AbstractV0.2 reclassifies A2 and separates reaction / field / trace layers

Problem. V0.1 named relation as reaction, but its chemical vocabulary carried unstable precision. Reaction drifted between event, capacity, absence, and inertial non-event; field drifted between parameter set, history, and trace container; chemical terms such as catalysis, corrosion, cleavage, precipitation, and inertia were not yet sufficiently distinguished from metaphor.

Approach. V0.2 introduces a terminology firewall. Reaction is split into capacity, event, inertial reaction, and no-reaction. Field is split into field-conditions, field-history, and field-trace layer. A2 is reclassified as a Field-Condition Hypothesis, not a founding axiom, until six parameters can be independently indicated.

Contribution. Relational Dynamics becomes a qualitative reaction-mechanics discipline: it does not replace relational sociology, attachment theory, chemistry, or field theory; it builds an operational vocabulary for bond-level change, trace formation, phase change, and relational catalysis under explicitly stated field conditions.

问题。V0.1 已经命名“关系即反应”，但化学词汇的精度尚未稳定：reaction 在事件、能力、无事件、惰性非事件之间漂移；field 在参数集合、历史容器、痕量承载层之间漂移；catalysis / corrosion / cleavage / precipitation / inertia 等词尚未与隐喻充分切开。

方法。V0.2 建立术语防火墙：反应被拆为反应能力、反应事件、惰性反应、无反应；场域被拆为场域条件、场域历史、场域痕量层；A2 从“公理”降级为 Field-Condition Hypothesis / 场域条件假说，直到六参数具备独立指明规则。

II · AxiomsA2 reclassified · A1/A3/A4 retained
Axioms and HypothesisV0.2 absorbs the strongest DeepThink pressure point
A1 · Reactivity Axiom
关系发生于可反应性，不等于每一邻近都已发生反应。
Relation presupposes reactivity: when two existents enter field proximity, a reaction-capacity is activated; whether a reaction-event occurs must be separately specified.
V0.2 repair: A1 no longer uses “inertial reaction” to absorb every no-reaction. A1 is a capacity axiom, not an event axiom.
H2 · Field-Condition Hypothesis
场域条件影响反应轨迹，但六参数必须被独立指明。
Field conditions modulate relational reaction. Temperature, pressure, concentration, energy, boundary, and medium remain semi-operational variables until independent indication rules are satisfied.
V0.2 repair: formerly A2. Downgraded from axiom to hypothesis because six parameters are not yet independently measurable. This is the survival repair of RD.
A3 · Bond-Change Axiom
真正的关系变化发生在 bond layer，而不只是标签层。
A relation changes when its bond configuration changes: strength, direction, reversibility, permeability, role, or threshold shifts.
Scope repair: this does not deny label changes; it declares that RD tracks bond-level change.
A4 · Trace Axiom
反应留下痕量；痕量有强弱层级。
Every reaction-event leaves trace, but trace must be graded: structural trace, informational trace, or field trace. Weak trace does not equal strong evidence.
V0.2 repair: A4 is no longer phrased as a universal conservation law. It is an irreversibility principle with graded trace layers.
M-Note · Position Non-Confusion
位置不可混淆是方法学注解，不是第五公理。
Position Non-Confusion instructs analysis not to collapse catalyst, reactant, medium, residue, and arbiter positions.
DeepThink integration: retained as method note, rejected as A5.
III · Reaction Splitreaction no longer absorbs no-reaction
Reaction Layer Splitthe repair that prevents A1 from becoming unfalsifiable
R1 · capacity
reaction-capacity / 反应能力
A field-proximity state in which reaction becomes possible. It is not yet an event.
R2 · event
reaction-event / 反应事件
A detectable relational shift: bond formation, weakening, acceleration, inhibition, phase change, or trace production.
R3 · inertia
inertial reaction / 惰性反应
Delayed or low-reactivity state under pressure. It must show resistance, delay, or stored potential; mere absence is not enough.
R4 · absence
no-reaction / 无反应
No detectable relational shift under the current frame. It is a legitimate result, not automatically renamed as inertia.
V0.2 锁定：无反应不是惰性反应的默认子集。 惰性必须被独立指明。
V0.2 lock: no-reaction is not automatically inertial reaction. Inertia must be independently indicated.
